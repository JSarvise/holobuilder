Solve ranks every 1 leader + 5 member team the chosen pool can field. It states its own cost as it starts, and where exact search can't finish it switches to a beam search and marks the result APPROXIMATE.

Inputs are files on disk: your collection from state/collection.json, the catalogue from data/cards.json, and your captured boards. Edit the first on Collection, the boards on Board, and pull them fresh with Refresh account.

  My collection — level, bloom, limit break
  

    

      Tell the tool what your cards actually are. Click a card, type its real
      level and bloom, then Save to disk — from then on the
      ranking scores your collection instead of a maxed one.
    

    

      Nothing you type here touches data/cards.json or
      data/owned.json. The only file this page writes is
      state/collection.json, and only when you press Save.
    

    
    

    

      How we know
      

        
data/cards.json carries fully-maxed stats for every
        card, so every ranking this tool produced before you edited here
        assumed a maxed collection. Levels, caps and blooms are resolved
        server-side by hbuilder.domain.collection and reported
        back — this page invents no bound of its own, so a level it
        accepts is a level the solver will honour.

      

    

    

      dumps/card_level_curve.json is missing, so no level can be resolved. Run
      python tools/recon/proxy/card_level_curve.py.
    

    

      
      
      
      
      
      
    

    
    

    

      

        
        
        
      

      

        
        
        
      

      

        
        
        
      

      

        Three numbers you read off your own account — the game shows the
        Power-Up figure on the Members list. An untouched slider means
        “not told us”, which is not the same as zero.
      

      

        How we know — and what each one changes
        

          
Posters and Power-Up are shown in the score
          breakdown and never summed into the ranking: MATH.md §7.2
          measured both as ranking-safe to drop, so they are reported rather
          than silently omitted.

          
Account level adds nothing to any score. It decides which
          Holomem Board nodes you may take at all — 127 of the 152 carry
          a level gate. Left unset, the board editor gates nothing.

        

      

    

    

    

  






  My board — nodes per character
  

    

      Plan a character's board. Pick a character, click nodes to spend points
      on them, then Save to disk. Clicking a node also buys every node
      on the path to it — that is what the game charges you.
    

    

      Your real board is captured from the game where a capture covers
      the character; editing here is planning on top of it. Each board says
      whether it is captured, edited or empty, and the three are never shown
      alike.
    

    

      How we know
      

        
A capture cannot give you two things. Planning:
        TREE-VERDICT.md §2 measured 478 points already earned and
        sitting unspent, 1.3× everything ever spent. And the
        28 of 54 characters that appear in no capture at all.

        
Adjacency is the orthogonal 4-neighbour grid (BOARD-SOCKETS.md
        §1, MEASURED — diagonals are not edges), and a node cannot be
        bought without its path: the 82 stat nodes cost 228 points in isolation
        and 293 connected. The figure below the picker is the connected
        cost, which is the one the game charges.
