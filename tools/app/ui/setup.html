holobuilder

The team optimizer for hololive Dreams, on this PC. Installs for your Windows user only: no administrator, nothing outside its folder but two shortcuts.

Your account

You pick a username: it is how the admin sees you. Google only confirms your email; your real name is not kept. Your game account and its file stay on this PC: the app reads them here and talks to the game directly. No IP address is kept.
