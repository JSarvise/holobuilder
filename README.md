## Getting started

1. Download `holobuilder-setup.exe` from [Releases](../../releases/latest) and run it.
2. Sign in with Google. Access is approved by the owner; the installer continues by itself once you're in.
3. Open holobuilder and point it at your game's account file (it usually finds it for you).

The app updates itself when a new version is out.

## Privacy

- Your game account file never leaves your PC.
- Your account data is read straight from the game, not through our server.
- We only keep the name and email from your Google sign-in. No IP addresses.
